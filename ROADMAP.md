## 🌟 Strategic Focus: Evidence Before Ecosystem

We prioritize **empirical engineering evidence** before scaling the ecosystem. Before expanding community skill submission, AgenticOS focuses on verifiable benchmarks proving reduced environment pollution, zero secret exposure, and consistent architectural integrity across coding agents.
